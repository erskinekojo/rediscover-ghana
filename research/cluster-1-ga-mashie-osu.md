# Cluster 1: Ga Mashie (Jamestown + Ussher Town) and Osu: research dossier (draft, November 2026)

**Purpose:** a starting point for the site and the shoot, built only from published sources found online. **Nothing here is final until Ga Mashie's quarters, Osu's traditional council and the people confirm it.** Confidence levels as in [teshie-research.md](teshie-research.md). Cluster context: [ga-dangme-clusters.md](ga-dangme-clusters.md).

**Honest note:** this cluster has the most written history, and the most **chieftaincy disputes.** I haven't resolved any of them; every claim below is stated as a claim. The first search for the quarters returned only Virginia's Jamestown, and I retried; **a list of quarters I gave from memory at first is now backed by two sources but spellings vary.** Several sources are tourism guides.

---

## 1. Ga Mashie: the basics
| Fact | Confidence | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Ga Mashie is "Old Accra", made up of **Ussher Town and James Town** (Jamestown) | High | [1][2] |
| Described as a **well-organised trading system by 1557** | Medium | [2] |
| **Seven traditional quarters**: **Abola, Asere, Akanmadze (Akumandje), Gbese, Alata (Ngleshie Alata), Otublohum, Sempe** | Medium–High | Two sources agree on the seven; **spellings vary** (Akanmadze/Akumadzen/Akanmajen/Akumandje; Alata/Ngleshie/Nlesh) [3][4] |
| **Asere, Abola and Gbese** are described as the oldest and "most traditionally Ga" | Medium | One source [3] |
| Colonial-era blocs: **Alata, Sempe and Akanmadze = "English Accra" / Jamestown**; **Otublohum and Gbese sat under Asere and Abola in "Dutch Accra"** | Medium | [3]. *Verify what Ga Mashie people say about this today* |
| Jamestown and Ussher Town are still **fishing communities, inhabited primarily by the Ga**; Ga and Fante workers were labourers at James Fort | Medium | [1] |

## 2. The forts, lighthouse and heritage
| Fact | Confidence | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **Ussher Fort** built **1649** (Dutch West India Company); after independence used as a prison **until decommissioned in 2008**; now a museum and documentary centre | Medium | [5] |
| **James Fort** built **1673 or 1673–74** (sources differ), first a trading post for gold, later for enslaved people | Medium | [5] |
| **Jamestown Lighthouse**: an original at James Fort in **1871**, current 28 m tower from the **1930s**; climbable by a narrow spiral staircase | Low–Medium | One travel source; dates differ. **Check before use** [5] |
| **Brazil House**: restored property, museum of Afro-Brazilian returnees; the ancestral home of the **Tabom people** | Medium | [5] |
| **Bukom** is described as a boxing district, with more than 20 gyms; **Azumah Nelson and Ike Quartey** named | Medium | [5] |
| "More boxers per capita than anywhere in the world" | **Low: a claim, not a statistic** | [5]. *Don't repeat it* |
| **Chale Wote Street Art Festival**, annual, in Jamestown | Medium | [5] |
| Jamestown is called "one of the cradles of Ghana's fight against colonialism" | Low–Medium | Graphic Online feature; **read it before using** |

## 3. Ga Mashie's chieftaincy and Homowo
- **The Ga Mantse stool is disputed.** My earlier note says the **Supreme Court in July 2025 affirmed King Tackie Teiko Tsuru II** [6]. Other reporting says the question has been **unresolved since the death of Boni Nii Amugi II in December 2004** and that **the government at one time recognised none of three claimants**; a regional House of Chiefs statement cautioned the feuding parties not to show publicly at Homowo. **Dates on that coverage are unclear and may be out of date.** [7] *Don't say who is the Ga Mantse unless an interviewee does, and give each side its say.*
- One 2026 report says that year's Homowo, "like many others before it, was celebrated without a Ga Mantse unanimously accepted by all our chiefs and people." [8] Low.
- **Sequence, as reported:** the **"Nea Wulumo" (highest priest of the Ga state)** sprinkles kpokpoi at selected family houses and historic sites early in the morning, followed by the **Gbese Mantse**, the Ga Mantse and other chiefs; in 2024 the Ga Mantse sprinkled kpokpoi at **21 houses representing the Ga stool**, with drumming, dancing and musket fire; in 2022 the Ga Mantse **visited the graves of past kings at Mantseman** to close the festival. [9] Medium.
- **The Dantu (Damte) priest** supplies the Ga calendar each year and **can shift it by a week or two**, by directive of the Ga Mantse or at his own discretion. [10] Medium. *(This is why Homowo dates are not fixed.)*
- **"Ye Yee Ye" / Akwele Suma Twin Festival:** a 2026 report says it begins Gamashie Homowo, to purify and appease **twin spirits**, always on the **Friday before the climax**. [8] Medium. *A beautiful and little-known ritual: **ask which families and what may be filmed.***
- Ga Mashie "marks Homowo a little earlier than La", with the climax in Ga Mashie. [11] Medium.

## 4. Osu
| Fact | Confidence | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **Christiansborg (Osu) Castle**: the Danes acquired the site in **1661** from **Okaikoi, the Ga paramount chief**, for **3,200 gold florins** (via governor Jost Cramer); Swedes had a lodge there the same year | Medium–High | [12] |
| It became the **Danish capital of the Gold Coast in 1685**; **sold to the British in 1850**; British governors ruled from it by 1876 | Medium–High | [12] |
| A major site of the **slave trade**: **tens of thousands** held in its dungeons until 1803, when Denmark enforced abolition (estimate) | Medium | [12] *Sensitive: treat with care and local voices* |
| **Ga-Danish mixed families** were prominent in Osu | Medium | [12] |
| An archaeological project draws on **oral histories from families around the castle** | Medium | [12]: a lead for the shoot |
| **Alata**: one source says the word is a corruption of **Allada** and came to name people from there; survives to identify Yoruba generally | Low | One source [12]. *Ask Osu's Alata families about this* |
| **Osu Homowo**: led by the **Osu Mantse**, central rite is **sprinkling kpokpoi**; the "**Nmaa Faa**" rites begin preparations | Medium | [13] |
| **Osu Mantse**: Nii Okwei Kinka Dowuona VI is named as Osu Mantse and paramount chief **in reports from about 2016–17** | Medium (dated) | [13] |
| **Disputed stool:** reports describe **rival claimants** (Nii Okwei Kinka Dowuona VI and Nii Nortey Owuo); one report says a **2006 Supreme Court ruling** favoured Nii Nortey Owuo III, a 2008 Kingmakers notice **says the opposite**. **Clashes at Homowo** reported | **Contested: conflicting reports** | [13] *Don't state a ruling. Check the current position with the Osu Traditional Council and court records* |
| Osu Alata Mantse: a photo caption names **Nii Kobina Boney** | Low | [13] |

**Not found:** the origin story of Osu's people, the meaning of "Osu", Klottey Korle, Osu's quarters. *Questions for the shoot.*

## 5. What's changing (Cluster 1)
- I could **not** find Ga Mashie-specific 2025–26 reporting on erosion, sea defences or redevelopment. [14]
- **Relevant nearby evidence:** a University of Ghana study of the **Dansoman sea defence** found it pushed fishers to relocate, and fisher protests changed the design from a wall to a groyne; a 2025 study found sea walls and groynes can worsen erosion in neighbouring communities. [14] *A good question for Ga Mashie's fishers: what has changed on the shoreline here?*
- National context: see [present-and-future.md](present-and-future.md).

## 6. Cultural overlaps with the three films
- **Fantasy coffins and funerals:** Ga Mashie has its own Jamestown funeral history (a University of Ghana thesis on Ga funerals in Jamestown exists). See [life-cycle.md](life-cycle.md).
- **Twin spirits** (the Ye Yee Ye festival) are a new thread for Part 3.
- **Boxing (Bukom)** and **Chale Wote** give the film a modern, young angle on Ga Mashie that the three eastern towns don't have.

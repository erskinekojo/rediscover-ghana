## 2. Where Nungua's people came from (disputed)
- **Official page (oral tradition):** the Krowor Municipal Assembly's website says the people of Nungua came from **"Tetetu" in Benin** with the first party of Ga, including some groups now in Ga Mashie [2]. **Medium** (it is the municipal government's own page, but it is oral tradition).
- **The priest Borketey Laweh:** the same page tells the story of the high priest **Borketey Laweh**, who was grievously wounded by an enemy after his own people failed to stand by him. He cursed the town, saying it would never grow, and **walked into the sea, where the waters parted**. The page says the town has since overcome the curse and prospered [2]. *Sensitive: tell it as the town tells it, and let an elder say how it is told today.*
- **The founder dispute:** a Newsghana response piece (read only via search summary) says descendants of Borketey Laweh claim he founded the Nungua state, and the author **rejects this**, crediting a son, **La Akotia**, as founder of the present town, with refugees and later newcomers joining [5]. **Low.** *There is a real argument here. Show accounts side by side, never ranked.*
- **The name:** one toponymy source reads "Nungua" as "purified water", from the Ga words for water and clean, and ties it to a search for clean water [6]. **Low.** *Ask the elders.*
- **First to arrive?** news reports say Nungua people are "said to be the first GaDangme group to arrive in present-day Ghana" and so are first to celebrate Homowo [7]. **Low** (a claim made at festival launches, not historical evidence).

## 3. Relationships with neighbours (all single-source, ask locally)
- **Nungua and Teshie:** by the Teshie family tradition, **Nungua's Mantse, Afote Okley, sold land to Teshie's founder Numo Nmashie** around 1710 [8]. Nungua's own account of this is not yet found.
- **La and Nungua:** one forum discussion describes a war in which **La defeated Nungua, after which the Kpeshie Lagoon became the boundary** between them [9]. **Low.** *Ask both towns' elders about this.*
- **Ga Mantse rites:** a report says the Ga Mantse's installation included **purification in Nungua** [10]. Medium (a news report). *Why Nungua? An excellent shoot question.*
- **Paramountcy:** Nungua, La and James Town were elevated to paramount status in November 2011 [11]. Medium.

## 4. Texture

### Kplejoo and Homowo
- **Kplejoo** is Nungua's planting festival, which comes **before Homowo**. It is described as the town's own: "unique to Nungua" in one source, though **Tema, Kpone and others also hold a Kplejoo** [12][13]. The Krowor Assembly says it falls on the **first Sunday in June** [2].
- In practice the festival is much longer: for 2026 the calendar had **22 customary events over three months** [14]. Rites include carrying **spring water to the sacred Gborbu grove**, cleansing the stools and praying for food and fish, and sprinkling kpokpoi at the **royal mausoleum** (the "Oheneasi" rites) [12][14].
- Titles you will hear: the **Gborbu Wulomo** (priest) assists the Nungua Mantse, Mankralo and other office-holders to receive the **holy corn** before the Homowo meal [14]. Medium.
- **Nungua is usually the first Ga town to begin Homowo** [14]. Medium. This pairs neatly with Teshie, which is last.
- The Nungua Traditional Council also performs the **"Jenten Nishwamo"** rite before Homowo [14]. *Ask what it means.*
- **Spelling:** Kplejoo / Kpledzoo / Kpledjoo all appear. Ask the Council which it prefers.

### Craft: Paa Joe's coffin workshop
- **Paa Joe** trained under Seth Kane Kwei in Teshie, then opened **Paa Joe Coffin Works in Nungua in 1976** [15][16]. **High.**
- **Important correction for visitors:** sources say he **moved the workshop to Pobiman** (Greater Accra) around **2007–2008** [15]. So the Nungua workshop is a historical landmark, not a place to visit today. **Medium** (sources differ by a year). Former apprentices trained at the Nungua workshop in the 1990s [15].
- Fantasy coffins are still used in funerals in Nungua [16].

### Food, fishing
- Nothing reliable found about a Nungua-specific dish or about fishing on Nungua's beach (the Wikipedia page notes only that **canoes are used on the beach**) [1]. *Ask locals.*

## 5. Getting there
- From **Circle (Odawna)**, a trotro to **Nungua** is listed (route 425) and Achimota and Abeka Lapaz routes run to Teshie-Nungua [17]. Nungua is on the **eastern rail line** [1]. **Medium.** Fares to be checked.
- Nungua is the neighbour you pass through en route to Teshie from Circle, so a combined Nungua + Teshie day is natural.
